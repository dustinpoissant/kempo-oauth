Deleting your data

If a provider gave you a confirmation code after you removed this site from your connected apps, enter it below to see what happened.

How to have your data deleted

  • Yourself: go to Delete your account. We email a six-digit code to the address on your account, and when you enter it your account is deleted.
  • Through Facebook: remove this site from the apps connected to your Facebook account. The site is told, deletes your account and its sign-in links straight away, and Facebook gives you a confirmation code to check here.
  • By email: write to the site’s operator from the address on your account, and ask for it to be deleted.

What is deleted, and what is kept, is described in the privacy policy.
